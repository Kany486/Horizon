## [Chinese Scientists Confirm Existence of Glueballs, New Form of Matter](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists at the Institute of High Energy Physics \(IHEP\), has announced the first experimental confirmation of glueballs after 15 years of research. They identified the particle X\(2370\), discovered in 2011, as a glueball-dominated state by determining its quantum numbers in 2024. This is the first observation of a particle made entirely of force-carrying gluons, a key prediction of the Standard Model&\#x27;s strong interaction theory \(quantum chromodynamics\). The result provides a crucial test of the Standard Model and opens a new window on the nature of the strong force. X\(2370\) was first seen in 2011 in J/ψ decays at BEPC-II, and its spin-parity quantum numbers \(0⁻⁺\) were determined in 2024 using a sample of 10 billion J/ψ particles. The team also observed several new decay modes and confirmed the particle&\#x27;s flavor-singlet nature, supporting its glueball interpretation.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: In particle physics, a glueball is a hypothetical composite particle consisting only of gluons, the force carriers of the strong interaction. Because gluons carry color charge, they can bind to each other without any quarks, a unique behavior predicted by quantum chromodynamics \(QCD\). BESIII, operating at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider \(BEPC-II\), has been a major facility for studying hadron spectroscopy, and the X\(2370\) emerged from decays of the charmonium state J/ψ, which produce gluon-rich final states.

## [Applying Pareto Frontier Analysis to Mario Kart Character Selection](https://www.mayerowitz.io/blog/mario-meets-pareto) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The article &\#x27;Mario Meets Pareto&\#x27; uses Pareto frontier analysis to examine Mario Kart character selection, showing how characters balance trade-offs between stats like speed and acceleration. It presents the concept of Pareto efficiency through a familiar gaming example. This matters because Pareto frontiers are a core concept in multi-objective optimization, used across engineering, economics, and software design. Using Mario Kart as an accessible example helps developers and decision-makers internalize how to evaluate trade-offs rather than chase single-metric optima. The analysis likely identifies which Mario Kart characters lie on the Pareto frontier for stats such as speed, acceleration, and handling, and which are dominated. **Background**: Pareto efficiency, also called Pareto optimality, describes a state where no objective can be improved without degrading another. In multi-objective optimization, the Pareto frontier \(or front\) is the set of all such efficient solutions, and it lets designers restrict attention to the meaningful trade-off candidates rather than every possible parameter combination. ## [Datasette 1.0a38 fixes SQL injection exposing private tables](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/6/datasette/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Datasette 1.0a38, released on August 6, 2026, fixes a SQL injection vulnerability that could let users with access to any public table read data from private tables in the same database via raw SQL queries even when the execute-sql permission is restricted. The fix is also included in Datasette 0.65.3. This security fix prevents potential data exposure in Datasette instances that serve a mixture of public and private tables, a scenario where the permissions system is essential. It is especially important for developers and site administrators who rely on Datasette&\#x27;s access controls to keep sensitive data safe. The vulnerability affects instances using the Datasette permissions system to configure mixed public/private access to tables within the same database. As a workaround, administrators are advised to disable the execute-sql permission on such databases until they upgrade to the patched versions.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 6, 18:24

**Background**: Datasette is an open-source tool for exploring, publishing, and analyzing data, with a built-in permissions system that controls actions like execute-sql, which allows users to run custom SQL queries. The execute-sql permission is enabled by default, and the vulnerability allowed bypassing this restriction to access private tables. The Datasette permissions system can be extended by plugins and managed through SQL-based rules.
