## [Kimi K3 Achieves Frontier Performance with Novel Engineering](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vaysjf/how_kimi_k3_engineered_its_way_to_the_frontier_r/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, an open-weight large language model that ranks fourth among 580 models on Artificial Analysis, featuring novel engineering breakthroughs: Kimi Delta Attention, Quantile Balancing for Mixture of Experts, and AgentENV microVM runtime for reinforcement learning. Kimi K3&\#x27;s innovations demonstrate that open-weight models can rival proprietary frontier systems, with breakthroughs in memory efficiency, expert load balancing, and RL training scalability that could accelerate research and democratize access to high-performance AI. Kimi Delta Attention replaces the KV cache in 69 of 93 layers with a single 128x128 matrix per head, reducing 1M-token context memory from 104.6 GiB to 27.2 GiB. Quantile Balancing keeps 896 experts per layer evenly loaded without auxiliary loss. AgentENV achieved 51 million sandboxes with 133 ms checkpoints and 49 ms resumes.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/noninertialframe96 · Jul 30, 16:37

**Background**: Large language models with long context windows typically require large KV caches that grow linearly with sequence length. Moonshot&\#x27;s Kimi Delta Attention, a variant of Gated DeltaNet, compresses this cache into a compact matrix, dramatically reducing memory. Mixture of Experts \(MoE\) models often suffer from unbalanced token routing; DeepSeek-V3 uses auxiliary-loss-based balancing, but Quantile Balancing from Kimi K3 computes bias from router score margins for stable training. Reinforcement learning for agents requires many isolated execution environments; AgentENV leverages Firecracker microVMs to provide fast, secure sandboxes with efficient pause/resume for inference.

## [MLVC: Multi-platform Learned Video Codec Debuts for Real-World Use](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vb3xwd/mlvc_multiplatform_learned_video_codec_for/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

MLVC is a neural video codec that achieves real-time performance on consumer NPUs from Apple, Intel, and Qualcomm while ensuring cross-platform entropy coding consistency by transmitting scale parameters through the hyperprior. This addresses a primary barrier—cross-platform interoperability—preventing learned video codecs from replacing traditional ones like H.264/H.265/AV1, potentially enabling widespread adoption of AI-based video compression. MLVC achieves &gt;70% MOS-based BD-rate improvement over hardware HEVC and averages ~100 FPS for both encoding and decoding on commodity NPUs at 540p resolution. The code is open-source on GitHub.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/tanelai · Jul 30, 19:40

**Background**: Learned video codecs use neural networks for compression but struggle with cross-platform deployment because small numerical differences can break entropy decoding. Traditional codecs like H.264 rely on deterministic hardware acceleration. MLVC avoids requiring bit-exact arithmetic by transmitting entropy model scale parameters through the hyperprior, which guarantees entropy coding consistency across devices.
